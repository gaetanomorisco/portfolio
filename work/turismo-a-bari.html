Editorial Design

Turismo a Bari

Year
2020
Type
University project
Role
Editorial design, infographics
Made
A4 booklet, 12 pages

A 12-page printed booklet proposing how Bari could attract visitors outside the summer, from October to December 2020.

It moves from the impact of COVID-19 on tourism, through data and infographics, to concrete strategies, ending with religious tourism around the Basilica of San Nicola.

I designed the editorial system: a teal and sand palette, serif headings, generous margins and full-bleed photography.
