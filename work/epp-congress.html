Event Identity

EPP Congress Helsinki 2018

Year
2018
Type

Agency
ZN Consulting, Brussels
Role

Made
Identity applications for the event

The visual identity for the EPP Congress 2018 in Helsinki, created by ZN Consulting in Brussels, where I was a design intern.

The idea is an “H” for Helsinki built from dots: a logo system that animates and adapts into icons, numbers and national flags, and runs across the stage screens, invitations, website and app.

As assistant to the art director, I helped apply the identity across the event’s materials.

Images courtesy of ZN Consulting. Creative direction: Jesus Azogue; art direction: Antonin de Bellefroid.
