nice to meet you

I'm Gaetano, a graphic designer focused on posters, advertising campaigns and brand identities.

I started designing at 14 and never really stopped. I went on to study Product Design, which taught me to think in systems, to prototype, and to care about how things are made and used. I now bring that way of working back to graphic design.

Along the way I rebranded Eazycity in Cork during an internship, and they still use that identity today. I also spent a short internship at ZN Consulting in Brussels, and designed my first freelance campaign, for CON TEMPO Festival 2019, printed and posted across the city.

Then I spent four years in aviation, as cabin crew and supervisor for Ryanair in Denmark and SAS in Sweden. It taught me to stay calm, precise and on time, in teams from all over the world. Now I'm back to design full time, and open to design roles and freelance projects.
